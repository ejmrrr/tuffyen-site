IMembership

What it offers, and why it’s different

Direct Georgia runs on Direct, a professionally managed platform built on more than two decades of venture investing. Two things set it apart: how you invest, and who you invest with.

01

The platform: how you invest

Invest alongside principals.

Direct sources the deals, runs the diligence and invests its own capital. You invest beside the team, never through a middleman.

A live pitch platform.

Interactive video sessions with founders and CEOs. Ask questions, commit in one click, and choose whether to take part under your name or anonymously.

Deal by deal, your call.

No blind fund. You choose which companies to back, if any, and build a portfolio to your own goals.

A professional back office.

Portfolio tracking, accounting, tax documents and a member portal handle the paperwork.

02

The community: why it’s different

The members are the edge.

375+ active members across 20+ states source and vet deals together, a reach no lone investor or small fund can match.

Every background counts.

High tech, science, engineering, professional services, the C-suite: each strengthens the group’s deal flow and diligence.

Contribute, or invest quietly.

Bring deals, join diligence and mentor founders, or stay passive and private. The choice is always yours.

Learning and events.

Investing courses, a guest speaker series, an annual symposium, dinners and curated company site visits.

One annual membership covers the platform, education, events and back-office services. You invest separately, deal by deal, and build a diversified portfolio across the companies the group has vetted.
