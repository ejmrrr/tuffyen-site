The Idea

Good investing is a group activity

Angel investing rewards a few simple disciplines: seeing enough good companies, judging them honestly, and investing across many rather than betting on one. Each is difficult to do alone. A single investor sees only the deals that happen to reach them, has only so many hours for homework, and can rarely build a portfolio wide enough to matter.

A group solves all three. It widens what you see, shares the work of judging it, and lets everyone build a sensible spread together.

The aim is not one lucky bet. It is a patient, disciplined portfolio, built with people who take it as seriously as you do.

That is what Seraph Georgia is for.
