What it offers — and why it’s different

Direct Georgia runs on Direct, a professionally managed platform with a 30-year track record. Two things set it apart: how you invest, and who you invest with.

The platform: how you invest

  • Invest alongside principals. Direct sources the deals, runs the diligence, and invests its own capital. You invest beside the team, never through a middleman.
  • A live pitch platform. Interactive video sessions with founders and CEOs. Ask questions and commit in one click, and choose whether to take part under your name or anonymously.
  • Deal by deal, your call. No blind fund: you choose which companies to back, if any, and build a portfolio to your own goals.
  • A professional back office. Portfolio tracking, accounting, tax documents, and a member portal handle the paperwork.

The community: why it’s different

  • The members are the edge. 500+ members across 20+ states source and vet deals together, a reach no lone investor or small fund can match.
  • Every background counts. High-tech, scientists, engineers, service providers, C-level: each strengthens the group’s deal flow and diligence.
  • Contribute, or invest quietly. Bring deals, join diligence, and mentor founders, or stay passive and private. The choice is always yours.
  • Learning & events. Investing courses, a guest speaker series, an annual symposium, dinners, and curated company site visits.
One annual membership covers the platform, education, events, and back-office services. You invest separately, deal by deal, and build a diversified portfolio across the opportunities the group has vetted.
